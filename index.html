1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this Agreement is to bring closure to the relationship, reduce misunderstandings, and help both Partners move forward in a healthy and respectful manner.

2. TERMS OF PARTING

2.1 Both Partners agree to end their romantic relationship and continue their lives as individuals.

2.2 Neither Partner is expected to continue romantic involvement unless both freely agree otherwise in the future.

2.3 Both Partners will respect each other’s privacy, personal space, dignity, and future relationships.

2.4 Any shared belongings, responsibilities, or commitments (if any) will be discussed and settled fairly and amicably.

2.5 Both Partners will aim to avoid harassment, threats, unnecessary conflict, and hurtful communication.

3. FINAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing below, each Partner confirms that they have read this personal agreement and are signing voluntarily. Each Partner understands that this is a personal relationship document, not a court order or a substitute for legal advice.
